# Citation

## Cite UOGW

> Midwest Stratospheric Data Systems (2026). Unified Open Global Weather (UOGW). https://github.com/Midwest-Stratospheric/Unified-Open-Global-Weather

## Always cite upstream sources

Examples:

- NOAA NCEI — IGRA, GHCNd
- NOAA NDBC — buoy observations
- Open-Meteo — city samples (CC BY 4.0)
- NASA GLOBE — citizen science protocols
- DWD, JMA, BOM, ECCC, and other national services when using their products
- ECMWF / Copernicus for open reanalysis products

UOGW is a **discovery and contribution layer**. Agencies and original projects remain the system of record for their observations.
