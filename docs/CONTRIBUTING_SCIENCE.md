# Contributing to UOGW Science

UOGW is curated by **Midwest Stratospheric Data Systems** as an open multi-layer atmospheric commons.

## Principles

1. **Agencies remain systems of record** — we index, sample, and attribute; we do not claim ownership of NOAA/NASA/ECMWF observations.
2. **Real data over empty catalogs** — daily commits under `data/entries/` must include observation payloads or research summaries.
3. **FAIR-minded** — stable paths, machine schemas, citation bundles, data dictionary.
4. **Safety** — anomaly detection is research screening, not public warning authority.
5. **Size discipline** — no multi-TB satellite granules in git; index open radiance paths instead.

## Highest-value contributions

- Additional open national met service indexes with clear licenses
- Quality-controlled HAB / near-space profiles from MSDS flights
- Validation notebooks comparing UOGW samples to independent stations
- Improved anomaly baselines (seasonal, station-specific)
- Documentation of variable conventions and uncertainty
