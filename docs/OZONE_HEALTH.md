# Ozone health snapshot

Research surface ozone & AQI-related fields from Open-Meteo air-quality API for Casey/Clark County and regional sample cities.

**Not EPA AirNow.** Official AQI: https://www.airnow.gov/

## Outputs

- `data/latest/ozone-health.json`
- `visuals/latest/ozone-casey-trend.png`

## Hub visual snapshots

`data/latest/hub-visual-snapshots.json` packages three views:

1. Heat & humidity (from science analytics + cities)
2. Marine NDBC waves/temps
3. Casey local hourly conditions
