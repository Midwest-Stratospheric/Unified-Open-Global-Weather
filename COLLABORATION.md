# Collaborating on UOGW

UOGW (Unified Open Global Weather) is the open atmospheric commons curated by Aerostratospheric, an Illinois nonprofit and home of Midwest Stratospheric Data Systems. We are looking for data collaborators: research groups, forecasters, classrooms, and software projects that read, extend, or check these layers.

## What is already public

1. The machine catalog at [`catalog/catalog.json`](./catalog/catalog.json), the daily [`science-package.json`](./data/latest/science-package.json), and the URL map in [`hub-endpoints.json`](./data/latest/hub-endpoints.json). The public tier needs no API key.
2. API documentation: https://www.midwestsds.com/data/v1/api.html and the developer overview at https://midwestsds.com/contact/knowledgebase.php?article=93
3. xDataHub, our public open atmospheric data dashboard: https://www.midwestsds.com/msds-data-hub.html. It carries the Casey, Illinois ground station KILCASEY47, NASA GLOBE air temperatures from the same site, a radiation index, NOAA KILX soundings beside our own flights, marine and upper air indexes, and the UOGW anomaly boards.
4. Flight products from our balloon missions. The Sep 12, 2026 x1Albatross envelope check reached 59,000 ft and the Sep 19, 2026 X2Griffon maiden flight burst at 90,356 ft. Archive: https://www.midwestsds.com/flights.html

## Kinds of collaboration we are looking for

1. Data collaboration: pairing your station, sounding, or model output with UOGW layers, or checking our derived products against yours.
2. Joint research or methods notes that use these files.
3. Classroom and STEM use, including xPlorer, our signed in learning dashboard with free 1 hour and 5 hour STEM certificates: https://www.midwestsds.com/explorer.html
4. Tooling: readers, notebooks, and visualizations built on the catalog.

We are not asking for donated gear or services. This is about working together on open data.

## Ground rules

Attribution is required. Curated indexes are CC BY 4.0, and original agency licenses still apply to source observations. Anomaly flags are research screens, not official watches or warnings.
